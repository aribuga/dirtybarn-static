  There are 3 different [fonts](https://dirtybarn.com/p-tag/font) in this collection. Splinter is sharp-edged, Dogshit is soft, and Iskele is in between.
  
  Splinter font is inspired by the sharpness in nature. The structure of the font is thick and pointy. The most repetitive feature in the font is the parts resembling a knife-like shape.
  
  Dogshit font is obviously inspired by the dog poop. It’s definitely organic so feel free to use it in your designs.
  
  Iskele font is inspired by scaffoldings – also a street name in Turkey – The structure of the font is a bit calligraphic-like and wavy.
