## 18\. Kobata

![](/media/posts/best-free-fonts-for-designers/image-18.jpg)

Kobata is an experimental typeface, designed by [Ariel Martín Pérez](http://arielgraphisme.com), based on some simple rules; firstly, the only possible shapes are rectangles and circles.  
Secondly, rectangles can’t touch each other on their sides to create more complex shapes, they can only meet by their corners. Lastly, Circles are the only shape of which the size can be optically corrected by diverting from the 50\*50 UPM grid.

[Download Kobata](http://www.tunera.xyz/fonts/kobata/)
