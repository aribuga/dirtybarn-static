There is an abundance of online and offline tools on the internet right now. Some are generally useful, while others target specific demographics. But one thing is for sure: it takes time and effort to separate the good ones from the others. Ultimately, only the individual knows what is useful for them. However, we took the extra steps and created a summary of productivity focused designer tools under four main categories:

## Productivity Focused Designer Tools:

1. Browser-based tools
2. Software that runs in the background
3. Multiplatform gems
4. Flavoured with a touch of AI

Naturally, there will be correlations between categories since every product aims to be convenient for every user. In such cases, we categorized them based on their strongest features.

*And lastly, tools are in no particular order in main categories and all descriptions were taken from the tools’ websites.*
