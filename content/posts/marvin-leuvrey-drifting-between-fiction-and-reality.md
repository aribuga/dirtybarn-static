Marvin Leuvrey is a Paris-based [photographer](https://dirtybarn.com/category/photography/) and artist. He has BA in Photography at ECAL/Ecole Cantonale d’Art de Lausanne and BA in Fine Arts at Ecole Nationale des Beaux-Arts de Lyon. He has collaborated with many brands such as Yves Saint Laurent, Margiela, Hermès, Virgil Abloh, Kenzo, Stella McCartney, Another Man, Wallpaper\*, Harper Bazaar Japan, L’Officiel Paris.

Marvin Leuvrey’s works are somehow technological and carry the concept of urbanity. The artist works with many different techniques and mediums such as analog, digital manipulation in photography, 3D, and video. The over-saturation in the artist’s photos sometimes feels like an illusion and adds a surreal feel. It is clear from the frames that the artist likes to focus on details so close that we can almost feel the texture. Marvin Leuvrey also manages to create two different realities and frames with his exposure games that even sometimes the images almost look like a collage. We can say that the photographs almost radiate their own aura, both literally and metaphorically.

We can say that Marvin Leuvrey likes to walk the line between reality and fiction while exploring the boundaries of the photographic medium. You can follow Marvin Leuvrey’s work from his [website](https://marvinleuvrey.com/) and Instagram [account.](https://www.instagram.com/Marvin.Leuvrey/)
